# AI Transformation Research

Cross-function research reading how AI adoption is progressing across enterprise operating functions, published by Open Future Forum.

## Reports

- [AI Transformation Report](https://openfutureforum.com/research/ai-transformation-report)

## About

This research reads AI transformation across functions on four markers: spend, headcount, work, and governance, drawing on first-party surveys and discussions from the Open Future Forum executive network.
