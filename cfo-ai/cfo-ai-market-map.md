—# CFO AI Market Map

**Publisher:** Open Future Forum
**Research type:** Market map / vendor landscape, paired with first-party demand data (Enterprise AI Buying & Budget Index, CFO lane)
**Audience:** CFOs, finance leaders, and finance AI vendors
**Edition:** Edition 1
**Publication date:** July 2026
**Canonical source:** https://openfutureforum.com/research/cfo-ai-market-map

## Overview

The CFO AI Market Map is a companion to the CFO AI Leverage Report. It maps the supply side of finance AI tools across eight CFO workflow areas (close and consolidation, FP&A and forecasting, AP and expense, treasury and cash, audit and controls, procurement and spend, tax, and the agentic/copilot layer), and pairs that map with first-party demand signals from Open Future Forum's finance-leader survey responses. Vendors are grouped editorially by workflow category; the report is explicit that vendors are not scored, ranked, or placed on a rated axis, and that no vendor paid for placement.

## Methodology & Sample

The demand overlay is drawn from seven application-stage survey instruments fielded around Open Future Forum sessions in 2026, totaling 421 reportable application-stage responses. Sample sizes vary by question: finance deployment stage (n=185), finance budget/sign-off/blocker/return-timing questions (n=76), a paired cohort chart (n=58), and founder buyer/pricing questions (n=49). Because several questions allow multiple selections, reported percentages are "any-mention" and can sum past 100%. The report states it avoids decimal precision on small bases and does not publish identifying respondent details.

## Key Findings

- 71% of finance-stage responses (131 of 185) reported already running Claude or another AI tool in their finance function, indicating deployment is ahead of budget clarity.
- Net-new budget money was cited in 35 of 76 finance budget responses, while 19 of 76 reported no clear AI budget line yet.
- Proving ROI was the most-cited buying blocker (40 of 76 responses), ahead of integration, security/compliance, data readiness, and talent.
- 47 of 76 respondents expect a measurable return within six months, favoring vendors who can demonstrate narrow, fast wins over broad platform pitches.
- Sign-off for AI purchases is cross-functional: the CEO appeared in 36 of 76 sign-off mentions, CFO/finance in 20, and CIO/CTO in 16.
- Among AI founders surveyed, CIO/CTO was named as the primary buyer in 23 of 49 responses and business-unit leaders in 17, versus only 5 naming CFO/finance — suggesting founders are not yet building go-to-market around the CFO seat.
- The agentic/copilot layer for finance shows early but directional interest, with the main hesitation centered on access control, audit trail, and human approval of agent-initiated actions rather than model quality.
