# CMO AI Market Map

**Publisher:** Open Future Forum
**Research type:** Market map / vendor landscape, paired with first-party demand data
**Audience:** CMOs, marketing leaders, and marketing AI vendors
**Edition:** Edition 1
**Publication date:** July 2026
**Canonical source:** https://openfutureforum.com/research/cmo-ai-market-map

## Overview

The CMO AI Market Map is a companion to the CMO AI Leverage Report. It maps the supply side of marketing AI tools across eight workflow areas (content generation, creative/brand/design, campaign and performance marketing, SEO/GEO/AEO, marketing analytics and attribution, lifecycle/email/CRM, social and community, and the agentic/copilot layer), paired with first-party demand signals from an Open Future Forum marketing and growth session. As with the other market maps, vendors are grouped editorially by workflow and explicitly not scored or ranked.

## Methodology & Sample

The demand overlay draws on 55 application-stage responses from an Open Future Forum marketing and growth session. Because the session mixed marketing/growth buyers with founders building AI products, the report separates the two groups: 16 of 55 respondents were builders (excluded from buyer findings and reported separately), leaving 39 buyer responses. The report notes this is a single-session read rather than a large probability sample, and frames its findings accordingly.

## Key Findings

- Among the 39 marketing buyers (after excluding builders), 29 were already piloting or running AI agents in production, and only 10 were still in the exploration stage — indicating marketing AI adoption in this community has moved well past early experimentation.
- 16 of 55 total session respondents were themselves building agentic AI products, reflecting how closely marketing's buy-side and sell-side communities overlap in the AI agent space.
- Open-text responses show marketing buyers asking for proof over promises: requests centered on seeing real production examples of marketing agents, the metrics they moved, and where they failed, rather than generic feature demonstrations.
- The vendor landscape includes a dedicated SEO/GEO/AEO (generative engine optimization / answer engine optimization) category, reflecting a shift in marketing tooling toward optimizing brand visibility inside AI-generated answers, not just traditional search results.
- Marketing analytics and attribution tools are increasingly framed around convincing finance that a specific spend decision changed, linking back to the broader CFO-finance scrutiny of marketing budgets described in the CMO AI Leverage Report.
