# Executive State of AI

Open Future Forum's annual flagship report. It answers who owns the AI buying decision, what AI has returned so far, and where AI budgets went in 2026, testing the year's most-cited AI research claims (BCG, MIT, PwC, Gartner, a16z, McKinsey, S&P Global, Menlo Ventures, KPMG, OECD, CB Insights) against first-party data.

## About

This is the annual flagship edition of Open Future Forum's Enterprise AI Buying and Budget Index. It synthesizes findings across the CEO, CFO, CMO, CISO, Executive, YC Founder, Investor, and VC & CVC research lines, drawing on 6,055 executive registrations across 15 Open Future Forum events collected April 22 through July 30, 2026.
