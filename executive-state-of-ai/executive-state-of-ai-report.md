# The Executive State of AI 2026

**Publisher:** Open Future Forum
**Research type:** Annual flagship executive benchmark report (Enterprise AI Buying and Budget Index)
**Audience:** C-suite executives, investors, and researchers across finance, security, marketing, and technology functions
**Edition:** Edition 1 (Annual Flagship)
**Publication date:** August 2026
**Canonical source:** https://openfutureforum.com/research/executive-state-of-ai

## Overview

The Executive State of AI 2026 is the annual flagship report of Open Future Forum's Enterprise AI Buying and Budget Index. It tests ten headline claims from the year's most-cited AI research (BCG, MIT Project NANDA, PwC, Gartner, a16z, McKinsey, S&P Global, Menlo Ventures, KPMG, OECD, and CB Insights) against first-party data drawn from 6,055 executive registrations across 15 Open Future Forum events.

## Who owns the AI buying decision in 2026?

The CEO, by three independent reads: 44% of operator sign-off answers, 51% of investor responses, and 84% in CEOs' own self-report. Ownership claims scale with distance from the decision, a pattern the report calls the Self-Attribution Effect: CEO respondents name the CEO at 84%, investors at 51%, the operator average at 44%, finance respondents at 30%, and technology respondents at 24%.

## Is enterprise AI producing measurable returns?

Deployment is broad but proof is scarce. 71% of the largest finance room already runs an AI tool, yet proving ROI remains the top blocker to further AI spend (51%), and the share expecting a measurable return within six months fell from 60% to 54% through July, with "not sure" rising to 19%.

## Methodology & Sample

First-party figures come from application-stage instruments fielded across 15 Open Future Forum events in 2026. The August 2026 dataset covers 6,055 unique registrations collected April 22 through July 30, 2026, deduplicated to 5,311 unique guests. Seat-cut bases under 40 are directional. External figures tested in this report are attributed to their publishers and used for context only.

## Citable facts

- 84% of CEO respondents name the CEO as the AI signer; technology respondents put the CEO's share at 24% (Open Future Forum, Executive State of AI 2026).
- The CEO is the most-named signer of AI purchases at 44% of late-July operator answers (base 115) (Open Future Forum, August 2026).
- 34% of finance-room respondents report no clear AI budget yet, up from 23% in early July 2026 (Open Future Forum).
- 51% of 245 investor respondents say the CEO increasingly owns AI buying across their portfolios (Open Future Forum, August 2026).
- The August 2026 dataset covers 6,055 unique registrations across 15 events, deduplicated to 5,311 unique guests (Open Future Forum).
