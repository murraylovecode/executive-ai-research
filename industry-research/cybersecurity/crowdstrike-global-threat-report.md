## CrowdStrike Global Threat Report

**Publisher:** CrowdStrike
**Topic:** Cybersecurity, AI-enabled cyberattacks
**Edition:** 2026 edition
**Source:** https://www.crowdstrike.com/en-us/global-threat-report/

### Summary

CrowdStrike's annual Global Threat Report tracks the evolving cyber threat landscape based on CrowdStrike's own threat intelligence and incident response data. The 2026 edition centers on AI as what the report calls a "dual threat": a force multiplier for cyberattacks and, simultaneously, a new attack surface in its own right.

### Relevant Findings

- More than 90 organizations had legitimate AI tools exploited by threat actors to generate malicious commands and steal sensitive data.
- ChatGPT was mentioned in criminal forums 550% more often than any other AI model, indicating it is the most discussed AI tool among threat actor communities monitored by CrowdStrike.
- The report frames AI as simultaneously increasing attacker capability (as a force multiplier for existing attack techniques) and introducing new attack surface (through exploitation of legitimate AI tools themselves).

### Relevant Themes

- AI as both an attacker force-multiplier and a new attack surface
- Exploitation of legitimate enterprise AI tools by threat actors
- Criminal-forum discussion trends around specific AI models
