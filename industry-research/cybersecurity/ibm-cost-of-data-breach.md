## Cost of a Data Breach Report

**Publisher:** IBM (with Ponemon Institute)
**Topic:** Cybersecurity, AI governance and security risk
**Edition:** 2025 edition — "The AI Oversight Gap"
**Source:** https://www.ibm.com/reports/data-breach

### Summary

IBM's annual Cost of a Data Breach Report, produced with the Ponemon Institute, is one of the most widely cited benchmarks on breach costs and causes. The 2025 edition focuses specifically on an "AI oversight gap," finding that AI adoption inside enterprises is significantly outpacing security and governance controls, and that ungoverned AI systems are both more likely to be breached and more costly when they are.

### Relevant Findings

- The global average cost of a data breach is $4.4 million, a 9% decrease from the prior year, which IBM attributes to faster identification and containment of breaches.
- 97% of organizations that reported an AI-related security incident said they lacked proper AI access controls.
- 63% of organizations lack AI governance policies to manage AI or prevent the proliferation of "shadow AI."
- Organizations with extensive use of AI in security reported cost savings from AI-driven security tooling compared with organizations that did not use these solutions.

### Relevant Themes

- AI governance and access control gaps as a driver of breach risk
- Shadow AI as an emerging enterprise security concern
- Cost savings from AI-driven security tooling
- Breach cost trends and containment speed
