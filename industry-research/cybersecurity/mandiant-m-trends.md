## M-Trends Report

**Publisher:** Google Cloud (Mandiant)
**Topic:** Cybersecurity, incident response, AI-abused attacks
**Edition:** M-Trends 2026
**Source:** https://cloud.google.com/security/resources/m-trends

### Summary

M-Trends is Mandiant's (Google Cloud) annual threat intelligence report grounded in its own incident response caseload, described in the 2026 edition as more than 500,000 hours of incident investigations conducted in 2025. The 2026 edition focuses on both emerging AI-driven attacker techniques and persistent systemic security failures that continue to account for most breaches.

### Relevant Findings

- Mandiant reports that the timeline for accelerated ransomware handoffs between cybercriminal partners has collapsed to as little as seconds in some observed cases, describing the overall window to intervene as having shrunk from hours to seconds.
- The report documents attackers actively abusing AI within compromised environments as an emerging technique, alongside more traditional attack methods.
- Modern extortion campaigns are shifting away from simple encryption toward "recovery denial" tactics designed to prevent victims from restoring systems even if they do not pay.
- Mandiant highlights threat actors targeting the virtualization stack directly to bypass guest-level defenses and embed deep, persistent access, along with systematic exploitation of edge devices and network appliances before patches are released.
- The report identifies SaaS integration abuse as a growing vector for large-scale data theft and cross-cloud lateral movement.

### Relevant Themes

- AI abuse within already-compromised enterprise environments
- Collapsing attacker timelines and the shrinking window for defensive intervention
- Recovery-denial extortion tactics beyond simple ransomware encryption
- Virtualization stack and SaaS integration abuse as emerging attack surfaces
