## Microsoft Digital Defense Report (MDDR)

**Publisher:** Microsoft
**Topic:** Cybersecurity, AI-enabled threats, nation-state and cybercrime trends
**Edition:** 2025 edition (published October 16, 2025)
**Source:** https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security/security-insider/threat-landscape/microsoft-digital-defense-report-2025

### Summary

The Microsoft Digital Defense Report is Microsoft's annual flagship threat intelligence report, drawing on signal from across Microsoft's security products and services. The 2025 edition frames cybersecurity as being reshaped by AI on both sides of the fight: threat actors are increasingly using AI to scale attacks, while defenders are using AI to compress response times and block large-scale fraud. Key themes include the growing use of AI by threat actors, the proliferation of infostealer malware, the growth of cybercrime-as-a-service, and the expanding role of nation-state threat actors.

### Relevant Findings

- Microsoft reports that defenders are already using AI to block billions of dollars in fraud and to compress incident response times from hours to minutes at scale.
- The report identifies AI-enabled threat actor activity, infostealer proliferation, and cybercrime-as-a-service as among the most pressing themes in the current threat landscape.
- Microsoft frames nation-state threat actors as playing an expanding role in the overall threat landscape alongside financially motivated cybercrime.
- The report's defensive recommendations center on strengthening identity and cloud resilience, disrupting criminal supply chains, and building stronger cross-industry and cross-government partnerships.

### Relevant Themes

- Dual-use of AI by both attackers and defenders in cybersecurity
- Cybercrime-as-a-service and infostealer malware trends
- Nation-state cyber threat activity
- Identity and cloud resilience as defensive priorities
