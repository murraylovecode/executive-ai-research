## Accenture Technology Vision 2025

**Publisher:** Accenture
**Topic:** Enterprise AI, AI agents, autonomy, technology strategy
**Edition:** Technology Vision 2025 — "AI: A Declaration of Autonomy" (January 7, 2025)
**Source:** https://www.accenture.com/us-en/insights/technology/technology-trends-2025

### Summary

Accenture's annual Technology Vision report identifies emerging technology trends shaping enterprise strategy. The 2025 edition centers on AI acting with increasing autonomy across enterprise systems, customer interactions, and robotics, introducing the concept of a "cognitive digital brain" that unifies workflows, institutional knowledge, and data into a system capable of higher-level autonomous action. The report argues that trust between employees, consumers, and autonomous AI systems will determine whether organizations can capture the full opportunity of this shift.

### Relevant Findings

- The report frames four emerging trends: exponential growth in generative AI reducing development costs and expanding digital agent autonomy; AI agents personalizing brand interactions at scale (raising brand differentiation challenges); foundation models embedded in robotics enabling more generalist physical-world tasks; and a "new learning loop" where generative AI diffused through an organization expands the autonomy of both employees and AI over time.
- Accenture argues that business leaders must proactively build trust with employees and consumers to realize value from increasingly autonomous AI systems, or risk losing the opportunity these capabilities present.
- The report positions AI agents as increasingly central to core enterprise technology architecture ("digital core"), not just point-solution tools.

### Relevant Themes

- Enterprise AI agent autonomy and governance
- Trust as a limiting factor in AI adoption
- AI-embedded robotics and physical-world applications
- Organizational learning and workforce transformation via AI
