## State of Generative AI in the Enterprise

**Publisher:** Deloitte (Deloitte Center for Integrated Research)
**Topic:** Enterprise AI investment, generative AI adoption
**Edition:** Quarterly survey series; third installment referenced here, fielded May–June 2024, 2,800+ respondents
**Source:** https://www.deloitte.com/us/en/insights/deloitte-insights-magazine/issue-33/ai-investment-opportunities-tech-ecosystem.html

### Summary

Deloitte's State of Generative AI in the Enterprise is a recurring quarterly survey tracking how organizations further along in gen AI adoption are directing related technology investment, including data management, cloud, cybersecurity, and traditional AI/ML spend alongside generative AI itself.

### Relevant Findings

- 70% of surveyed leaders are investing in data management capabilities as a prerequisite for successful AI/gen AI strategies.
- 73% expect cloud consumption investment to increase alongside gen AI investment.
- Cybersecurity co-investment averaged 75% across industries, with financial services, energy/resources/industrials, and government especially focused on increasing cybersecurity spend to support gen AI initiatives.
- 53% of respondents expect investment in traditional AI/machine learning to increase alongside gen AI investment, suggesting many organizations plan to combine predictive and generative approaches.

### Relevant Themes

- Enterprise AI/gen AI investment prioritization
- Data, cloud, and cybersecurity as AI-enabling infrastructure spend
- Industry variation in AI-related technology investment
