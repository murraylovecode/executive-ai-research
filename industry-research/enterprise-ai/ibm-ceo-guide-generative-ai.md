## The CEO's Guide to Generative AI

**Publisher:** IBM Institute for Business Value (in collaboration with Oxford Economics)
**Topic:** Enterprise AI, CEO decision-making, generative AI strategy
**Edition:** Ongoing multi-chapter research series (latest chapter referenced: "Cost of Compute," October 2024)
**Source:** https://www.ibm.com/thought-leadership/institute-business-value/en-us/report/ceo-generative-ai

### Summary

The CEO's Guide to Generative AI is an ongoing, multi-chapter research series from the IBM Institute for Business Value examining how CEOs are approaching generative AI investment, risk, and organizational change. It draws on two proprietary surveys: one of 200 US-based CEOs and another of 369 executives across the US, UK, India, Singapore, and Germany, both fielded in April–May 2023, and is expanded over time with chapters on specific functional areas (talent, customer service, cybersecurity, marketing, finance, and more).

### Relevant Findings

- 64% of CEOs report facing significant pressure from investors, creditors, and lenders to accelerate generative AI adoption.
- Investment in generative AI is expected to grow nearly four times (4x) over the following two to three years, though it remained a fraction of total AI spend at time of survey.
- 60% of organizations are not yet developing a consistent, enterprise-wide approach to generative AI, despite the acceleration pressure.
- The report frames CEO generative AI decisions as high-stakes trade-offs between strategic advantage and risks including data privacy, legal liability, and bias.

### Relevant Themes

- CEO-level generative AI strategy and investment
- Enterprise-wide AI governance and consistency gaps
- Risk management in AI adoption (privacy, bias, explainability)
