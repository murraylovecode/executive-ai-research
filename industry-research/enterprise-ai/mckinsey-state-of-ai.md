## The State of AI: McKinsey Global Survey

**Publisher:** McKinsey & Company (QuantumBlack)
**Topic:** Enterprise AI adoption, AI agents, organizational transformation
**Edition:** "The State of AI in 2025: Agents, Innovation, and Transformation" (published November 5, 2025)
**Source:** https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/quantumblack/our-insights/the-state-of-ai

### Summary

McKinsey's recurring Global Survey on the state of AI tracks enterprise AI adoption, scaling, and business impact. The most recent edition finds AI use continuing to broaden across organizations, with growing experimentation in AI agents, but most organizations still early in scaling AI to capture enterprise-level value.

### Relevant Findings

- 88% of respondents report regular AI use in at least one business function, up from 78% a year earlier, but nearly two-thirds say their organization has not yet begun scaling AI across the enterprise.
- 62% of respondents say their organizations are at least experimenting with AI agents; 23% report scaling an agentic AI system somewhere in the enterprise, most often within only one or two functions.
- Only 39% of respondents report any enterprise-level EBIT impact from AI, and most of those attribute less than 5% of EBIT to AI use — even as a majority report qualitative gains in innovation.
- Larger companies scale faster: nearly half of respondents from companies with more than $5 billion in revenue report reaching the scaling phase, versus 29% of companies with under $100 million in revenue.
- Respondents are split on AI's effect on workforce size: 32% expect decreases, 43% expect no change, and 13% expect increases over the coming year.

### Relevant Themes

- Enterprise AI adoption and scaling maturity
- AI agents and early enterprise deployment
- Gap between AI use and measurable enterprise financial impact
- Organization size as a driver of AI scaling speed
