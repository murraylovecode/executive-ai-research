## Microsoft Work Trend Index

**Publisher:** Microsoft (WorkLab)
**Topic:** Enterprise AI, workforce productivity, AI agents
**Edition:** 2026 — "Agents, Human Agency, and the Opportunity for Every Organization" (May 2026)
**Source:** https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/worklab/work-trend-index/agents-human-agency-and-the-opportunity-for-every-organization

### Summary

The Work Trend Index is Microsoft's recurring research series on how AI is changing work, based on large-scale global employee surveys. The 2026 edition, based on a 10-market survey of roughly 20,000 respondents fielded in early 2026, examines how organizations can capture the expanded individual agency that AI agents create. It finds organizational factors matter far more than individual behavior in determining whether employees realize real value from AI.

### Relevant Findings

- Organizational factors (culture, manager support, talent practices) accounted for more than twice the statistical association with real AI value (67%) compared with individual mindset and behavior (32%).
- The single strongest factor identified was an organization's AI culture, roughly two and a half times as strong a signal as the leading individual-level factor.
- The report frames AI impact as environment-dependent: whether managers model AI use, encourage experimentation, and whether talent evaluation reflects AI use, matters more than individual skill or attitude.

### Relevant Themes

- Enterprise AI adoption and organizational readiness
- Manager and culture effects on AI value realization
- AI agents and the changing nature of individual work
