## PwC AI Jobs Barometer

**Publisher:** PwC
**Topic:** Enterprise AI, labor market, wages and skills
**Edition:** 2026 Global AI Jobs Barometer — "Two Futures for Jobs in an AI Era"
**Source:** https://www.pwc.com/gx/en/services/ai/ai-jobs-barometer.html

### Summary

PwC's AI Jobs Barometer analyzes job postings and workforce data to track how AI is reshaping labor markets. The 2026 edition argues that AI is creating a "two-track" labor market, in which companies most exposed to AI see substantially higher productivity growth and are raising wages and headcount faster than companies with less AI exposure, while skill demands shift much faster in the most AI-exposed roles.

### Relevant Findings

- Productivity growth is 40% higher at companies most exposed to AI compared with companies least exposed.
- Skills required for the most AI-exposed jobs are changing more than twice as fast (2x) as for the least AI-exposed jobs.
- Jobs "professionalized" by AI are growing twice as fast as jobs "democratized" by AI, with 42% faster wage growth since 2021 for the professionalized track.
- The most AI-exposed junior roles are seven times (7x) more likely than the least AI-exposed junior roles to require traditionally senior skills such as leadership.

### Relevant Themes

- Labor market bifurcation driven by AI exposure
- Wage and productivity effects of AI adoption
- Changing skill requirements in AI-exposed roles
