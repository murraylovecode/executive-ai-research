## Stanford AI Index Report

**Publisher:** Stanford HAI (Institute for Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence)
**Topic:** Enterprise AI, AI research trends, economic and societal impact
**Edition:** 2026 AI Index Report
**Source:** https://hai.stanford.edu/ai-index

### Summary

The Stanford AI Index is an annual, independent, data-driven report tracking AI's technical progress, investment trends, adoption, and societal impact across sectors. It is widely cited by media, governments, and policymakers as a comprehensive reference point for the state of AI globally. The 2026 edition highlights a widening gap between AI capability and the governance/measurement frameworks needed to manage it, alongside continued acceleration in enterprise AI adoption and investment.

### Relevant Themes

- AI adoption and investment trends across industries
- AI governance and measurement frameworks
- Divergence between technical capability and organizational readiness
- Global comparisons of AI sentiment and policy response
