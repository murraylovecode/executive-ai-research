## Deloitte CFO Signals: AI Governance and Risk Management

**Publisher:** Deloitte
**Topic:** CFO/Finance AI, AI governance, risk management
**Edition:** Q2 2026 CFO Signals survey (published July 23, 2026)
**Source:** https://www.deloitte.com/us/en/insights/topics/business-strategy-growth/2q-2026-cfo-signals-survey.html

### Summary

Deloitte's CFO Signals is a recurring quarterly survey of North American CFOs at large organizations (US$1 billion+ revenue). This edition, surveying 200 CFOs, focuses on AI governance confidence and risk concerns as AI use has moved from experimentation to broad deployment within finance functions.

### Relevant Findings

- 93% of respondents say their organizations use AI extensively or modestly across multiple key functions, up sharply from three years earlier when 66% said their companies were still just experimenting with or discussing generative AI.
- Only 43% of CFOs feel confident in their organization's current AI governance; 53.5% feel only somewhat confident.
- 59% cite balancing pressure to deploy AI quickly against managing risk as the biggest challenge in building an effective AI governance framework.
- 46% name cost uncertainty or lack of billing transparency (often due to consumption-based AI pricing) as their top internal concern; 43% cite litigation risk from use of protected/private content as their top external concern, and 41% cite cybersecurity.
- 33% of CFOs say their CISO owns AI governance at their organization, while 19% say the CFO holds the greatest AI governance responsibility — ahead of the CEO (12%), an AI governance committee (8%), and the board (4.5%).
- Within finance itself, 51% of CFOs use AI for operational productivity tasks, 44% for financial planning and budgeting, and 41% to analyze financial data for insights.

### Relevant Themes

- CFO confidence in AI governance frameworks
- Cost transparency and consumption-based AI pricing risk
- Cross-functional ownership of AI governance (CISO vs. CFO vs. CEO)
- AI use within the finance function itself
