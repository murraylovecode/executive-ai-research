## 7 Finance Activities AI Will Reshape Most by 2030

**Publisher:** Gartner
**Topic:** CFO/Finance AI, finance function transformation
**Edition:** Gartner Finance Insights article (publicly accessible summary; full research available to Gartner clients)
**Source:** https://www.gartner.com/en/articles/ai-finance-transformation

### Summary

Gartner's finance research practice argues that AI represents the most significant change to the finance function since the digital spreadsheet, and outlines a roadmap for CFOs to move from legacy systems and mindsets toward an AI-first finance function by 2030.

### Relevant Findings

- Gartner recommends CFOs redesign finance teams, roles, and ways of working specifically to capture AI-driven productivity gains, rather than simply layering AI tools onto existing workflows.
- The guidance emphasizes validating finance AI use cases and applying structured best practices for identifying, prioritizing, and piloting new AI opportunities, including ongoing assessment of emerging vendors.
- Gartner frames build-versus-buy as a central decision for CFOs, urging finance leaders to understand where vendor capabilities lead or lag internal expectations before deciding what to source externally versus build in-house.
- The research ties AI roadmap decisions directly to measurable ROI, explicitly linking automation and efficiency gains to potential headcount displacement as a financial outcome to plan for.

### Relevant Themes

- Finance function redesign around AI-first workflows
- Build vs. buy decisions for finance AI vendors
- ROI measurement and headcount planning tied to finance AI adoption
