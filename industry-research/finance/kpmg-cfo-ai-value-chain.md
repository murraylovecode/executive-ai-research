## Leading the AI Value Chain (Voice of the CFO)

**Publisher:** KPMG US
**Topic:** CFO/Finance AI, finance transformation, talent strategy
**Edition:** Voice of the CFO Insight Series, 2026
**Source:** https://kpmg.com/us/en/articles/2026/cfo-leading-ai-value-chain.html

### Summary

Part of KPMG's recurring "Voice of the CFO" insight series, this article argues that CFOs are shifting from traditional reporting and control functions toward becoming strategic partners driving enterprise-wide AI adoption. It frames AI-driven productivity gains as large enough to require a fundamentally different CFO mindset, moving from incremental improvement to bold organizational change.

### Relevant Findings

- KPMG reports AI can deliver productivity gains as high as 50 to 70 percent in finance functions, which the firm argues requires CFOs to pursue enterprise-wide change rather than incremental process improvement.
- The article identifies five key CFO priorities for AI leadership: championing AI projects aimed at revenue growth or major cost reduction (not just efficiency), modernizing data and re-engineering core financial processes, expanding the CFO's strategic remit alongside the CEO, redesigning talent strategy (managed services, project rotations, new training models) to prepare finance teams for automation, and defining business outcomes before selecting AI/technology investments.
- KPMG frames the CFO's evolving role as requiring less time on routine reporting tasks and more time on strategic influence and partnership with business leaders.

### Relevant Themes

- CFO-led enterprise AI strategy and value chain transformation
- Finance talent redesign in response to AI automation
- Outcome-first approach to AI and technology investment decisions
