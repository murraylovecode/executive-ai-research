## Oracle AI Agents for Finance (Oracle Fusion Cloud Applications)

**Publisher:** Oracle
**Topic:** CFO/Finance AI, AI agents in finance operations
**Edition:** Announced at Oracle AI World, October 15, 2025
**Source:** https://www.oracle.com/news/announcement/ai-world-oracle-ai-agents-help-finance-leaders-accelerate-business-insights-and-boost-efficiency-2025-10-15/

### Summary

This is a product and vision announcement rather than a survey-based research report: Oracle detailed new AI agents embedded in Oracle Fusion Cloud ERP and EPM applications aimed at automating core finance workflows. It is included here as a vendor perspective on where finance AI agent capability is heading, distinct from the first-party survey research elsewhere in this collection.

### Relevant Findings (vendor-reported)

- Oracle introduced four finance-specific AI agents: a Payables Agent (automating multi-channel invoice processing, matching, and fraud/policy checks), a Ledger Agent (natural-language monitoring, auto-created adjustment journals), a Planning Agent (real-time trend/variance analysis and what-if simulation for FP&A), and a Payments Agent (optimizing cash outflows, early pay, and virtual card programs).
- Oracle reports that more than 11,000 organizations across nearly every industry and geography use Oracle Fusion Cloud ERP, giving these agents a large existing enterprise finance user base.
- The agents are positioned as natively embedded and included at no additional cost within Oracle Fusion Applications, rather than sold as standalone add-ons.

### Relevant Themes

- Vendor-side view of agentic AI in core finance workflows (AP, ledger, FP&A, payments)
- Embedded AI agents vs. standalone AI tooling in enterprise finance software
- Working capital and close-cycle impacts of finance AI agents
