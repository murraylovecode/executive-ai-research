## What's Important to the CFO in 2026 (PwC CFO Priorities)

**Publisher:** PwC
**Topic:** CFO/Finance AI, finance transformation, capital allocation
**Edition:** 2026 CFO priorities
**Source:** https://www.pwc.com/us/en/leadership-center/cfo.html

### Summary

PwC's CFO leadership hub outlines five trends framing the finance leader's 2026 agenda, drawing on PwC's broader CEO Survey and "Value in Motion" research. It frames CFOs as enterprise strategists balancing AI investment against margin protection, with "embed AI into finance delivery" named as one of five explicit 2026 focus areas alongside strategic capital allocation, finance transformation, risk/regulatory resilience, and financial reporting transparency.

### Relevant Findings

- Only 30% of CEOs report being very or extremely confident about revenue growth over the next 12 months, per PwC's 29th Global CEO Survey (of more than 4,000 business leaders), even as many commit capital to AI and business model reinvention — a tension PwC frames as central to CFO decision-making in 2026.
- PwC's "Value in Motion" research estimates $7.1 trillion in revenue will shift this year as companies reinvent business models to unlock growth, which the firm frames as directly relevant to CFO capital allocation decisions.
- PwC explicitly names "embed AI into finance delivery" as one of five top CFO focus areas for 2026, alongside strategic capital allocation, finance transformation, risk/regulatory resilience, and financial reporting transparency.

### Relevant Themes

- CFO capital allocation amid AI investment pressure
- Finance transformation and AI embedded in finance delivery
- Business model reinvention and revenue-pool shifts
- CFO role as enterprise strategist
