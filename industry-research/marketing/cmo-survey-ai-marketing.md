## The CMO Survey: Marketing Contracts Under Economic Pressure Despite Growing Value and AI Gains

**Publisher:** Deloitte, Duke University Fuqua School of Business, and the American Marketing Association (The CMO Survey)
**Topic:** CMO/Marketing AI adoption, marketing spend, organizational readiness
**Edition:** 35th edition (published March 31, 2026)
**Source:** https://cmosurvey.org/marketing-contracts-under-economic-pressure-despite-growing-value-and-ai-gains/

### Summary

The CMO Survey is a long-running biannual survey of marketing leaders at U.S. for-profit companies, produced jointly by Deloitte, Duke Fuqua, and the American Marketing Association. This edition surveyed 308 marketing leaders (97% at VP-level or above) amid rising economic pessimism, finding that AI use in marketing is accelerating even as organizational readiness lags adoption.

### Relevant Findings

- AI use in marketing has more than tripled since 2022, with generative AI adoption growing especially fast.
- Companies project AI will account for more than half of all marketing activities within three years.
- Measurable improvements in sales productivity, customer satisfaction, and marketing overhead costs from AI have all risen year over year.
- No marketing technology activity scores above 5 on a 7-point performance scale, including integrating marketing technologies into the customer funnel and generating ROI from marketing technologies — indicating adoption is outpacing readiness.
- Marketing training budgets have fallen to just 3.8% of marketing spending, down from a pre-pandemic high of 5.8%, even as marketing capabilities are rated critical to business success.

### Relevant Themes

- Gap between AI adoption speed and organizational readiness
- Marketing technology ROI measurement challenges
- Underinvestment in marketing capability-building despite AI transformation
- CMO-CFO partnership and marketing's expanding mandate
