## AI Is Reshaping Marketing, And CMOs Must Lead The Transformation

**Publisher:** Forrester
**Topic:** CMO/Marketing AI leadership, operating model transformation
**Edition:** Blog analysis published March 16, 2026 (Rani Salehi, Principal Analyst)
**Source:** https://www.forrester.com/blogs/ai-is-reshaping-marketing-and-cmos-must-lead-the-transformation/

### Summary

Forrester analyst Rani Salehi argues that AI adoption functions as a stress test for marketing leadership rather than a shortcut to progress, exposing structural gaps in decision rights, ownership, and incentive alignment that organizations must resolve to scale AI successfully.

### Relevant Findings

- Forrester's research finds that when organizations rush AI adoption without addressing unclear decision rights or fragmented ownership, pilots linger and fail to scale.
- The analysis recommends CMOs explicitly define AI's role in the operating model, decide which decisions AI should accelerate, and align governance and incentives before scaling AI use.
- A featured industry perspective from Cisco's VP of marketing strategy and AI initiatives frames success with AI as being about empowering people with the right tools and clarity, rather than replacing them.

### Relevant Themes

- AI adoption as an organizational readiness test
- Marketing operating model redesign for AI
- Leadership clarity, governance, and incentive alignment
