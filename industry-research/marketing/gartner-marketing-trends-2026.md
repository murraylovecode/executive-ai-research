## Gartner Marketing Trends 2026: Future of Marketing

**Publisher:** Gartner
**Topic:** Marketing/CMO AI, agentic AI, marketing organization design
**Edition:** Marketing predictions article, published December 2025
**Source:** https://www.gartner.com/en/articles/future-of-marketing

### Summary

Gartner's annual marketing trends article lays out five predictions for how AI reshapes the marketing function over the next several years, from agentic AI handling one-to-one customer interactions to a shift toward composable, AI-dependent marketing organizations.

### Relevant Findings

- Brands are predicted to adopt agentic AI to handle many routine one-to-one customer interactions, such as notifications, reorders, and personalized outreach.
- Influencer budgets are expected to shift toward creator and content authenticity as AI-generated media accelerates and social-originated content increasingly dominates search results.
- CMOs are predicted to move toward fully composable, AI-dependent marketing organizations, flattening structures around modular teams with blurred role boundaries.
- A growing ecosystem of AI-enabled wearables, sensors, and connected devices is expected to become a major channel for brand experiences, shifting engagement from explicit search toward ambient, context-driven interactions.
- Despite industry optimism, Gartner predicts GenAI shopping tools will generate less than 10% of e-commerce revenue, as consumer trust in autonomous purchasing agents remains limited.

### Relevant Themes

- Agentic AI in customer engagement
- Marketing organization design and composability
- AI-generated content and creator economy shifts
- Consumer trust limits on autonomous commerce
