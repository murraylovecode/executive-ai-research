## The 2026 State of Marketing Report

**Publisher:** HubSpot
  **Topic:** Marketing/CMO AI, brand strategy, AI adoption in marketing workflows
**Edition:** 2026 edition
  **Source:** https://www.hubspot.com/state-of-marketing

### Summary

HubSpot's annual State of Marketing report surveys marketers on AI adoption, brand strategy, and the balance between automation and human-led marketing. The 2026 edition frames AI as now being a baseline expectation rather than a differentiator, with growth increasingly driven by a distinct brand point of view and audience trust.

  ### Relevant Findings

  - 61% of marketers believe marketing is experiencing its biggest disruption in 20 years due to AI.
  - 80% of marketers use AI for content creation, and 75% use it for media production.
  - Marketers rank brand awareness campaigns, customer experience alignment with brand promises, and brand messaging framework development among the branding investments delivering the highest ROI.
  - The report's authors argue AI has become table stakes, and competitive advantage now comes from how well marketers operationalize it rather than simply whether they use it.
  - A featured HubSpot marketing executive cautions that most AI-generated content is average quality, and that audiences increasingly seek out human-created content in gated spaces like newsletters, podcasts, and video.

  ### Relevant Themes

  - AI as baseline capability rather than differentiator
- Brand point of view as a growth lever
- Balancing AI efficiency with human-led creativity and trust
