## Global Private Equity Report 2026

**Publisher:** Bain & Company
**Topic:** Private equity, AI-driven value creation
**Edition:** 2026 edition — "Powering Forward in a New Era"
**Source:** https://www.bain.com/insights/topics/global-private-equity-report/

### Summary

Bain's Global Private Equity Report is one of the industry's most widely cited annual benchmarks on dealmaking, fundraising, and value creation trends. The 2026 edition describes a "K-shaped recovery" in which deal and exit values surged in 2025 (helped by a handful of megadeals) while distributions to investors remained low and fundraising stayed difficult for many general partners. The report argues that with cheap debt and easy multiple expansion no longer available, GPs must build systemic, data-backed value creation capabilities, explicitly including AI investment, to hit higher return targets.

### Relevant Findings

- Bain frames 2026 as requiring GPs to treat "12 as the new 5" — meaning deals now require substantially faster EBITDA growth than in prior eras to hit target returns, since multiple expansion and cheap leverage can no longer be relied upon.
- The report explicitly names investment in talent and AI as core components of the value-creation systems winning firms are building, moving from "full potential diligence" at the deal stage to actual execution starting Day 1 of ownership.
- Deal and exit values surged in 2025, but the recovery was narrow (driven by megadeals) and distributions to limited partners remained stubbornly low, creating continued fundraising difficulty for many GPs.

### Relevant Themes

- AI as a core lever in private equity value-creation strategy
- Post-megadeal, K-shaped recovery in PE deal activity and fundraising
- Shift from diligence-stage planning to Day-1 execution of value creation plans
