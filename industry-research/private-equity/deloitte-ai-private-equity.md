## AI in Private Equity: Transforming the Technology Stack

**Publisher:** Deloitte
**Topic:** Private equity, AI-driven technology transformation, portfolio company value creation
**Edition:** Published June 23, 2026 (part of Deloitte's "Operate to Transform" perspective series)
**Source:** https://www.deloitte.com/us/en/services/consulting/articles/ai-in-private-equity-technology-transformation.html

### Summary

This Deloitte perspective addresses what it calls the "modernization paradox" in private equity: portfolio companies need agentic AI and modern technology capabilities to compete, but building that capability requires capital, specialized skills, and effort that most PE organizations don't have in-house, all while running the existing business without disruption. Deloitte proposes an "Operate to Transform" managed-services model designed to sequence AI/technology transformation so that early value creation helps self-fund the rest of the transformation.

### Relevant Findings

- Deloitte frames AI-driven technology transformation in PE portfolio companies as facing a "modernization paradox": the skills needed for do-it-yourself transformation are often not present in-house, and it is difficult to overhaul a technology stack while simultaneously running business as usual.
- The proposed solution sequences transformation phases so that early proceeds/value fund ongoing investment, described as flattening the spending curve, reducing up-front capital outlay, and making the overall AI transformation self-funding rather than requiring large upfront investment.
- Deloitte frames this as following PE's core "buy, improve, sell" formula applied to technology capability itself: bringing in managed services during a transformation period, then transferring enhanced AI/technology capability back to in-house management once value has been created.
- Deloitte notes it was recognized as a Leader and Star Performer in Everest Group's Private Equity Services PEAK Matrix Assessment 2026, for two consecutive years.

### Relevant Themes

- Financing models for AI transformation in PE portfolio companies (self-funding, phased approaches)
- Managed services as a bridge for AI capability building in PE-owned companies
- Agentic AI as a competitive requirement for portfolio companies
