## EY Private Equity Pulse

**Publisher:** EY (Ernst & Young)
**Topic:** Private equity, AI-led disruption in technology investment
**Edition:** Q1 2026 edition
**Source:** https://www.ey.com/en_us/insights/private-equity/pulse

### Summary

EY's Private Equity Pulse is a recurring quarterly update tracking private equity deal activity and sentiment. The Q1 2026 edition highlights a sharp shift in PE sentiment toward the technology and software sector specifically, driven by concerns about AI-led business model disruption, with investors becoming markedly more selective about which software assets they back.

### Relevant Findings

- Technology accounted for approximately 30% of global PE deployment by value in the prior year, but fell to just over 10% of deployment value in Q1 2026 — a sharp reallocation away from tech.
- EY estimates that a "normal" level of tech investment in Q1 2026 would have produced roughly 12% year-on-year growth in aggregate PE deployment; instead, aggregate PE deployment declined by about 12%, implying the tech pullback alone reversed the quarter's overall growth trajectory.
- Generalist PE investors are placing elevated emphasis on diversification away from concentrated tech exposure, while specialist investors are emphasizing their ability to distinguish between software business models well-positioned to benefit from AI versus those at risk of AI-driven disruption.

### Relevant Themes

- AI-driven reallocation of PE capital away from software/technology
- Investor differentiation between AI beneficiaries and AI-disrupted business models
- Quarterly PE deployment trends and sector concentration risk
