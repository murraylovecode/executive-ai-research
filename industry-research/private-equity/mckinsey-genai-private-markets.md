## Harnessing the Power of Gen AI in Private Markets

**Publisher:** McKinsey & Company
**Topic:** Private equity, generative AI in investment research and due diligence
**Edition:** Article published January 5, 2026 (part of McKinsey's Global Private Markets Report 2026 series)
**Source:** https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/private-capital/our-insights/harnessing-the-power-of-gen-ai-in-private-markets

### Summary

This McKinsey article examines how private markets investors are using generative AI and large language models (LLMs) to accelerate investment research, and cautions that today's obvious productivity gain could become tomorrow's diligence failure if not paired with proprietary, expert-driven data. McKinsey compared LLM-generated "deep research" reports against expert interview-based research across multiple industries to quantify where gen AI outputs diverge from ground truth.

### Relevant Findings

- 67% of investors believe gen AI will have a "transformational" impact on their business within five years, and 82% view its use as a high priority, according to survey data cited in the article.
- Across more than 100 structured queries spanning multiple industries, McKinsey found gen AI deep-research reports were more optimistic ("happy talk" bias) than expert-interview-based reports in seven out of ten industries analyzed.
- LLM-generated reports often diverged meaningfully from expert-interview findings on core investment metrics including market size, growth rates, pricing dynamics, and margin structures.
- About 40% of important data points found in expert interviews were entirely absent from corresponding LLM answers on the same topics, even with additional prompting — including deal-critical details like contract structures, unit economics, and regulatory hurdles.
- In a case example (US baby/kids' apparel market), the gen AI report significantly overstated online sales penetration and understated per-child spending compared with expert-interview-based research, and incorrectly suggested the market had shrunk over the prior decade.

### Relevant Themes

- Risks of over-relying on public LLMs for investment due diligence
- Combining proprietary/expert data with gen AI outputs for balanced diligence
- "Happy talk" bias and data omissions in AI-generated market research
- Process discipline needed to safeguard AI-accelerated deal research
