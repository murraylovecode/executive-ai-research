## Private Equity: US Deals 2026 Midyear Outlook

**Publisher:** PwC
**Topic:** Private equity, AI-enabled portfolio company value creation, deal market bifurcation
**Edition:** Published June 17, 2026 — "A Tale of Two Markets: PE's Bifurcation Accelerates"
**Source:** https://www.pwc.com/us/en/industries/financial-services/library/private-equity-deals-outlook.html

### Summary

PwC's midyear 2026 US private equity deals outlook describes a market that entered the year expecting a dealmaking recovery but instead saw escalating geopolitical tensions and rapidly accelerating AI capabilities add new uncertainty, further suppressing the exit activity sponsors need to return capital to limited partners. PwC frames the result as a market defined less by aggregate pace and more by widening dispersion between firms that can demonstrate realized returns and those that cannot. A companion piece identifies five data-driven trends PE firms can use to unlock AI-enabled potential in portfolio companies.

### Relevant Findings

- PwC frames rapidly accelerating AI capabilities, alongside geopolitical tensions, as a direct contributor to continued uncertainty and suppressed exit activity in the 2026 PE market, rather than the more straightforward recovery many expected entering the year.
- The report frames 2026 PE performance as increasingly bifurcated: dispersion between top and bottom performing firms is widening, with realized returns (not just paper markups) becoming the key differentiator.
- PwC's companion research on portfolio companies focuses on using data and AI readiness, alongside operational improvements, as levers to grow margins, revenue, and ultimately exit value.

### Relevant Themes

- AI capability acceleration as a source of market uncertainty and valuation dispersion in PE
- Bifurcation between PE firms with realized returns versus those without
- Portfolio company AI readiness as a value-creation lever
