# Investor AI Report

**Publisher:** Open Future Forum
**Research type:** Investor benchmark report (venture, growth, and private equity portfolios)
**Audience:** Venture capital investors, growth investors, private equity investors, corporate and strategic investors
**Edition:** Edition 1
**Publication date:** August 2026
**Canonical source:** https://openfutureforum.com/research/investor-ai-report

## Overview

The Investor AI Report adds a new vantage point to the Enterprise AI Buying and Budget Index: investors reading AI's buying decision and impact across entire portfolios rather than inside a single company. Edition 1 draws on 245 responses collected largely at Open Future Forum's Investors Summer Drinks event.

## Who owns the AI buying decision across portfolios?

51% of investors name the CEO as the seat that increasingly owns AI buying across their portfolio companies, more than double the second answer, the CIO/CTO at 24% (base 245). 22% say it is too early to call.

## Where is AI making the biggest difference in portfolios?

Investors point to offense over defense: better products lead at 51%, ahead of cutting costs at 35% and helping customers at 34% (base 237). 16% report nothing measurable yet across most of their portfolio.

## How does the investor view compare with operators?

Ownership claims scale with distance from the decision: CEO respondents name the CEO at 84%, investors at 51%, the operator average at 44%, finance respondents at 30%, and technology respondents at 24%.

## Methodology & Sample

First-party reads come from an application-stage instrument embedded in Open Future Forum's investor events, beginning with Investors Summer Drinks (July 27, 2026). This edition draws on 245 responses to the ownership question and 237 to the portfolio-impact question. Any-mention convention applies to multi-select questions; bases under 40 are directional. This is a first edition and single-event base, treated as a baseline rather than a trend.
